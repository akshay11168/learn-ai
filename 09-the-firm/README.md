# 09 — The firm

A firm is the legal and operational shell around consulting you already know how to deliver. It does not create clients, judgment, or a delivery method. Those come from paths 07 and 08. Open the shell when a client needs to contract an entity, when liability should not sit on you as a private person, or when you are invoicing often enough that a professional tells you the current arrangement is the wrong one.

Every lab here produces a question list, a policy, or a checklist. Several of the answers must come from a lawyer and a chartered accountant who practice in your country and know this year's rules. This course cannot give you those answers. Company types, tax registration, employment law, and what insurance you need change by country and over time. Treat the labs as the preparation you do so those meetings are short and specific.

Do not incorporate on the day you finish path 08. Finish the rehearsal. Take one paid pilot if you can, even a small one, under a simple agreement a lawyer has seen. Then use that experience to fill these labs with real gaps instead of hypothetical ones.

## Standards that sit underneath

- **Separation.** Client files, your personal projects, and the firm's money are different places. Mixing them is how small firms lose trust and create tax messes.
- **Writing.** The SOW, the invoice, and the handover describe the same work.
- **Limits.** You do not quietly become the client's compliance department, law firm, or overnight operations team.
- **Record of claims.** Marketing text on a website cannot outrun the case studies. If the site says you do medical AI and the wedge says you classify support tickets, the site is wrong.

## Done with this path when

You have a written trigger for forming an entity, a briefing document you could email a lawyer and an accountant, a data policy, one repeatable offer, and a clear statement of what the first hire would do. Whether you have filed paperwork is a decision those professionals help you time. The course is done when the decision is informed.
