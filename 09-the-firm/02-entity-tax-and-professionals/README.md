# 02 — Entity, tax, and the professionals

Prerequisite: [01 When to form the firm](../01-when-to-form-the-firm/README.md).

## Purpose

Prepare a briefing so a chartered accountant and a lawyer can advise you in one meeting each. You are not choosing a company type from a blog post. You are learning the names of the decisions so you can hear their advice.

Nothing in this folder is tax, legal, or corporate advice. Rules depend on your country, your state, and the current year.

## Explain before you code

1. Why might the right first step be "keep operating as an individual, open a separate bank account" rather than "register a company"?
2. What goes wrong when business expenses and household expenses share one account for a year?
3. Which questions are for the accountant, and which are for the lawyer?

## Decisions they will make with you

Write `brief.md` with your facts filled in, and leave the recommendation blank for the meeting.

**Facts you provide**

- Country and city where you live and where the clients are likely to be.
- Whether any client is outside your country.
- Expected revenue for the next year as a range, marked as a guess.
- Whether you will hire in the next year.
- Whether client data includes personal data.
- The wedge and the pilot description.

**Questions for the accountant**

- Should this be a sole proprietor, a partnership, a limited company, or some other local form, and why, at your guessed revenue?
- When does tax registration apply (names differ: GST, VAT, sales tax, and others)? Ask them to check the current threshold. Do not copy a number from memory or from this course.
- How should you invoice, in which currency, and which records do you keep?
- What may you deduct, and what must you not run through the business?
- How do you pay yourself from the entity if you create one?

**Questions for the lawyer**

- What liability stays with you personally even after you incorporate?
- What contract form do they want for a small fixed-price pilot?
- What licenses or professional rules apply to advising clients in your category, if any?
- If you incorporate, what filings and ongoing duties come with that choice?

**If you are in India.** Ask the accountant in those words about proprietorship versus a private limited company versus an LLP, about GST and invoicing, and about whether a separate current account is due now. Ask the lawyer about the contract and about personal liability. Do not treat a general article as an answer. Thresholds and forms change.

## Build

Complete `brief.md` with your facts and the question list. Add the names of two accountants and two lawyers you could actually email, with why you picked them (a referral, a relevant practice, a city). Sending the email can wait until a trigger in lab 01 is close. The brief should be ready before that week.

## You are done when

The brief contains your facts, separates the two professionals, and leaves their recommendation blank rather than filled with a guess from the internet.
