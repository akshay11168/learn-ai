# 01 — When to form the firm

Prerequisite: [the consulting rehearsal](../../08-consulting/06-rehearse-an-engagement/README.md).

## Purpose

Decide what "opening the firm" is for, and what it does not fix. The usual mistake is to spend the month after learning AI on logos, a domain, and a company registration, before anyone has agreed to pay for a pilot.

## Explain before you code

1. Which client problems are solved by a registered company, and which are solved by a finished SOW?
2. Why is a personal project labeled as a client engagement a trust failure, not a marketing shortcut?
3. What can you deliver next month as a sole practitioner that a firm would deliver in the same way?

## Triggers that justify the paperwork

Write `triggers.md` and mark which of these are true now. Forming an entity becomes reasonable when one or more are true and your accountant agrees the timing is right:

- A client requires a company on the contract, or their vendor process cannot pay an individual.
- You are taking on liability you do not want in your personal name (a system that influences a costly business process). Their lawyer or yours has said the contract needs an entity.
- Money is arriving often enough that separating business and personal accounts is the accountant's advice, not your aesthetic preference.
- You are about to hire or subcontract.
- A procurement form asks for insurance that is sold to businesses.

These are not triggers:

- You finished the course.
- You want a business card.
- A social post said founders should incorporate early.
- You have not yet done discovery with a real buyer.

## Build

1. `triggers.md` with the list above, dated, and your yes or no.
2. A one-paragraph description of the practice you would wrap: the wedge sentence from path 08, the pilot length, and the fact that you are pre-revenue or post-pilot. Be exact.
3. The next commercial action that is not paperwork: send the rehearsal to a friend for the reading test, ask for a discovery conversation, or complete a paid sample of labeling work. Name the action and a date.

## You are done when

You can say either "not yet, because the trigger is a client" or "yes, because this specific trigger fired," and the next action on the calendar matches that sentence.
